Sweets in Heritage Chowk

At Shiv Mishthan Bhandar, located in the vibrant heart of Heritage Chowk, our mithai tradition spans across generations. Every morning before dawn, our master halwais kindle traditional brass cauldrons to churn pure cow milk into fresh khoya, paneer, and malai. We take immense pride in preparing iconic delicacies like Kaju Katli, Motichoor Ladoo, and Ghee Mysore Pak strictly adhering to time-honored recipes without artificial preservatives or vegetable fats. Whether you are celebrating festivals, family weddings, or simply craving an authentic piece of heritage sweetness, our sweets bring unmatched purity and heartfelt devotion directly to your table.
